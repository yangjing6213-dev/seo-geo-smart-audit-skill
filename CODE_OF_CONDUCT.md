# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include demonstrating empathy, respecting differing viewpoints, giving constructive feedback, accepting responsibility, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or imagery, insulting or derogatory comments, harassment, publishing others' private information, and other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces and when an individual officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report abusive behavior privately through the repository's GitHub Security Advisory form. Maintainers will respect the reporter's privacy and determine proportionate corrective action. Do not include unnecessary personal data.
